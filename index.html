Yo Agami | Official Website

Yo Agami is a real, independent music artist. His new album Stereo 80 comes out on November 25, 2026. It has 8 tracks. His music is on Spotify and he is on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube as @itsyoagami. AGM (Agami Global Management) is his own independent label and management.

This official website is an interactive retro 1980s computer called YO-OS. Explore the desktop: the Stereo 80 album app with a daily countdown, a music player, original mini games like NightDrive, Grand Theft Audio, Snake, Mixsweeper and a drum machine, a paint app, a terminal, and a built-in browser with a whole fake 80s internet (search engine, radio station, video site, shop and more). Hidden files, secret pages and puzzles are waiting for curious visitors.
